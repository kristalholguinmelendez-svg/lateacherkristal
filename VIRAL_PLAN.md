# Viral plan: LA Teacher Kristal

**Positioning:** The LA teacher who fixes the English mistakes Spanish speakers make *without noticing*.
One niche, said the same way in every bio: "Inglés real de Los Ángeles para hispanohablantes."

## The engine
1. **Short video** (TikTok / Reels / Shorts) hooks people with one mistake.
2. **The quiz** (`quiz/reto.html`) is the link in bio and the CTA: "Haz el reto, link en mi perfil."
3. People share their score, which brings in new people. That's the loop.
4. Pinned comment and bio link lead to classes / WhatsApp.

## 4 repeatable formats (film 5 per sitting)
| Format | Structure (≤30s) | Why it spreads |
|---|---|---|
| **"Deja de decir…"** | On-screen: ❌ *I have 25 years* → ✅ *I'm 25*. Say it, explain in 1 line. | Saves + "I didn't know" comments |
| **Falso amigo** | "Si dices *embarrassed* en EE.UU. creen que…" pause, reveal. | Shock and humor get shares |
| **Calle de LA** | Ask strangers / coworkers a quiz question on camera. | Real reactions, you're the LA teacher |
| **Corrijo tu inglés** | Stitch/duet viewers' videos and fix one phrase kindly. | Free reach from their audience; people send you clips |

## Rules that actually move the numbers
- **First 1 second:** text on screen plus a strong statement. No "Hola chicos, hoy les voy a…"
- **Captions always** (Descript or CapCut). Most people watch muted.
- **End with a question** for comments: "¿Cuál de estos errores cometías tú?"
- **Post daily for 30 days**, same time (7–9pm US Pacific works for US Latinos). Consistency beats perfection.
- **Reply to comments with video.** Every reply is a new post.
- **Recycle winners:** if a video beats your average 3×, make 3 variations of it the same week.
- **Bilingual on purpose:** speak Spanish, show English on screen. That's the niche.
