# Reel 01: "My thoughts exactly"

**Length:** 20–25s · **Format:** 9:16, face to camera · **Hook mistake:** "I'm agree"

| Time | On screen (text) | You say / do |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | ❌ "I'm agree" | Point at camera, serious face: "Deja de decir *I'm agree*." |
| 2–5s | 🚨 | Wrong-answer buzzer sound. "Suena a traducción y los gringos lo notan." |
| 5–9s | ✅ **My thoughts exactly** | Smile, slow and clear: "Mejor di: *My thoughts exactly.*" |
| 9–12s | = Pienso exactamente lo mismo | "Significa: pienso exactamente lo mismo." |
| 12–17s | 🗣️ mai **TH**ots eg-ZAKT-li | "Pon la lengua entre los dientes en *th*: *thoughts*. Repite conmigo." Pause 1s. |
| 17–21s | Friend: "Monday should be illegal." · You: "My thoughts exactly." 😂 | Act out both sides (turn head left/right). |
| 21–24s | Comenta 👇 "My thoughts exactly" | "Si estás de acuerdo, comenta *My thoughts exactly*." |

**Why it works:** the CTA makes people practise the phrase *in the comments*, and comments push reach.

**Caption:**
Deja de decir "I'm agree" 🛑 Di esto y suena natural 🇺🇸
Comenta "My thoughts exactly" si ya lo sabías 👇
📲 Haz mi reto de inglés: link en mi perfil

**Filming tips:** natural light facing a window, phone at eye level, burn in captions, cut every pause. Use a trending sound at 10% volume under your voice.

**Follow-up reel (same week):** use the green screen to show a real comment and reply "My thoughts exactly" with a better alternative: *"Couldn't agree more"* / *"Totally"* / *"Same here"*.
